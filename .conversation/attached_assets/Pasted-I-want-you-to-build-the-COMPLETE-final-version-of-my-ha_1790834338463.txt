PROJECT NAME:
AccessPath

TAGLINE:
"Maps tell you where to go. AccessPath tells you whether you can get there — and how."

HACKATHON THEME:
AI for Accessibility & Inclusion

PROBLEM:
Normal maps can tell people where a place is, but they often do not tell users whether they can actually access that place based on their individual accessibility needs.

People with mobility, visual, hearing, cognitive, or other accessibility requirements may encounter stairs, blocked ramps, unavailable elevators, inaccessible entrances, confusing paths, inaccessible washrooms, or outdated accessibility information.

AccessPath should help users:
1. Understand accessibility conditions of campus locations.
2. Describe their accessibility needs in natural language.
3. Find a route suitable for those needs.
4. SEE that route visually on an interactive map.
5. Compare alternative routes.
6. Report accessibility barriers.
7. Use AI to interpret accessibility needs and barrier reports.
8. Keep a shared campus accessibility record updated.

TARGET DEMO:
A user wants to travel from "Main Gate" to "Computer Lab".
The shortest route may contain stairs.
The user says:
"I cannot use stairs and I don't mind walking a little farther."

The system should understand this and recommend a step-free route.

The user must then be able to click "View route on map" and see the actual route drawn visually on an interactive campus map.
